Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Data we access

  • From TikTok, after you log in and consent: your basic profile (open ID, nickname, avatar) and the access tokens needed to upload and publish videos to your own account.
  • Publication information returned by TikTok (publish ID and status).

How the data is used

Only to show which account is connected and to upload or publish the videos you choose, with the settings you choose. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer (a local file). Streamer clips has no server and no database; nothing is sent to anyone other than TikTok's official API.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect. Clicking Disconnect in the application deletes them immediately. You can also revoke access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
