Terms of Service

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. The service

Streamer clips is a personal desktop application that helps its owner prepare short clips from public live streams and publish them to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.

2. Account and consent

Connecting a TikTok account requires the account owner to log in on TikTok's official page and grant permission. The application never asks for or stores a TikTok password. Every publication requires a manual click from the owner; there is no automatic posting.

3. Content

The owner is responsible for the content published, and must respect TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, Twitch's Terms of Service and the rights of the creators whose streams are clipped. Clips are short excerpts with added subtitles and editing, credited to the original creator.

4. Availability

The application is provided "as is", without warranty. It may stop working if TikTok or Twitch change their services.

5. Termination

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in the application or by removing the app in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
