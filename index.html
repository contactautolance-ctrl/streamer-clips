Streamer clips

Streamer clips is a personal desktop application (Windows) used by the TikTok account @clips.vicety to prepare short vertical clips of public Twitch live streams (gaming, mostly GTA 6) and publish them to that account.

How it works

  1. The application watches selected live streams and suggests short moments (vertical format, subtitles, short on-screen hook).
  2. The owner reviews every clip manually. Nothing is ever published automatically.
  3. The owner logs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and, for each clip, chooses the privacy level and interaction settings, then clicks Publish. The video is sent with the TikTok Content Posting API, either as a draft to the TikTok inbox or as a direct post with its caption.

TikTok features used

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): show which TikTok account is connected (nickname and avatar).
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): send a clip to the TikTok drafts inbox.
  • Content Posting API (video.publish): publish a clip with its caption and hashtags, after the owner's explicit click.
